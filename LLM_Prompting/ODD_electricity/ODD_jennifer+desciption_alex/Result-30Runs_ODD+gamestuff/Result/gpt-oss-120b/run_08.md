| 1 | **Capacitor‑Coordination Game** – Farmer A ↔ Farmer B (same transformer) | “Invest in a voltage‑stabilising capacitor” vs “Do not invest”. | 2 | **Connection‑Authorization Choice** – Farmer ↔ Sub‑station staff | Farmer decides **Authorized** vs **Unauthorized** electricity connection. | 3 | **Informal‑Collusion Negotiation** – Farmer ↔ Sub‑station staff | Both parties may engage in a **reciprocal informal exchange** (e.g., bribe, favour). | 4 | **Transformer‑Capacity Investment Dilemma** – Representative Farmer ↔ Sub‑station staff | Farmer can **Contribute** (pay part of a capacity upgrade) or **Free‑Ride**. | 5 | **Groundwater‑Extraction Race** – Farmer A ↔ Farmer B (same aquifer) | Each farmer chooses **High Extraction** (maximise current yield) or **Restrain** (conserve water). | 6 | **Enforcement‑Compliance Game** – Sub‑station staff ↔ Farmer | Staff selects **High Enforcement** (intensive monitoring, higher effort) or **Low Enforcement**. | 7 | **Pump‑Quality Selection under Inspection** – Farmer ↔ Sub‑station staff | Farmer decides **Standard‑Approved Pump** (higher upfront cost) or **Low‑Quality Pump** (cheaper). | 8 | **Social‑Learning Imitation Decision** – Early‑Adopter Farmer ↔ Neighboring Farmer | Early‑adopter decides **Adopt Capacitor** or **Not Adopt** (first‑mover).
