**Summary**

The ten action situations above capture every distinct local strategic tension that the ODD + D narrative makes explicit:

1. **Capacitor coordination** (farmer‑farmer).  
2. **Transformer‑capacity contribution** (farmer‑farmer free‑riding).  
3. **Collusive informal exchange** (farmer‑staff).  
4. **Enforcement vs. tolerance** (staff‑farmer).  
5. **Staff capacity‑investment decision** (staff‑farmer group).  
6. **Groundwater extraction commons** (farmer‑farmer).  
7. **Pump‑set quality externality** (farmer‑farmer).  
8. **Authorization processing** (farmer‑staff).  
9. **Staff workload‑maintenance trade‑off** (staff‑staff).  
10. **Learning‑driven imitation of technology** (farmer‑farmer).

Each matrix or game‑tree respects the ordinal payoff logic, the bounded‑rational, socially‑learned behaviour, and the explicit coordination/competition mechanisms described in the model documentation.
