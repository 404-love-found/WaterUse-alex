### Action Situation 1: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Coordination Dilemma
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
|  | Farmer 2 Adopts | Farmer 2 Does Not Adopt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopts** | 3, 3 | 1, 2 |
| **Farmer 1 Does Not Adopt** | 2, 1 | 2, 2 |
#### Justification: Farmers face a dilemma in deciding whether to adopt capacitors for voltage stability. If both adopt, they both benefit (3, 3). If one adopts and the other does not, the adopter bears the cost without full benefit (1, 2 or 2, 1). If neither adopts, they both miss out on the potential benefit (2, 2).

### Action Situation 4: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Common-Pool Resource Management
#### Tension: Over-Extraction Dilemma
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 chooses extraction level (High or Low).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and chooses extraction level (High or Low).
3. Payoffs are determined by the combination of choices, with over-extraction leading to decreased payoffs for both.
#### Justification: Farmers sequentially decide on groundwater extraction levels, facing a dilemma of over-extraction. The first farmer's choice influences the second farmer's decision, with both seeking to maximize their payoff without considering the long-term sustainability of the resource.

### Action Situation 5: Social Learning and Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Technology Diffusion
#### Tension: Information and Coordination Dilemma
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 adopts or does not adopt a capacitor.
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's outcome and decides to adopt or not.
3. Payoffs depend on the adoption decisions and the observed outcomes, with successful adoption encouraging further adoption.
#### Justification: Farmers face a dilemma in adopting new technology based on social learning. The first farmer's adoption and its outcome influence the second farmer's decision, with the potential for diffusion of the technology if the outcome is positive, and stagnation if the outcome is negative or unclear.
