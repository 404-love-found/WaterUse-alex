### Action Situation 1: Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Coordination for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination Dilemma
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 2 Adopts | Farmer 2 Does Not Adopt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopts** | 3, 3 (Improved reliability) | 1, 4 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |
| **Farmer 1 Does Not Adopt** | 4, 1 (Farmer 1 free-rides, Farmer 2 bears cost) | 2, 2 (No improvement in reliability) |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the dilemma farmers face when deciding whether to adopt capacitors. The payoff matrix illustrates the strategic tension between farmers, where mutual adoption leads to improved voltage stability, but unilateral adoption can result in one farmer bearing the cost while the other free-rides.
