### Action Situation 1: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Coordination dilemma among farmers to adopt capacitors for improved voltage stability
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 2 Adopts Capacitor | Farmer 2 Does Not Adopt Capacitor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopts Capacitor** | 3, 3 (Improved voltage stability for both) | 1, 4 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |
| **Farmer 1 Does Not Adopt Capacitor** | 4, 1 (Farmer 1 free-rides, Farmer 2 bears cost) | 2, 2 (No improvement in voltage stability) |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between two farmers deciding whether to adopt capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that mutual adoption leads to the best outcome (improved voltage stability), but unilateral adoption can result in one farmer bearing the cost while the other free-rides.
