#### Title: Coordination for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors to improve voltage stability, with benefits depending on neighboring farmers' actions.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
|  | Farmer 2 Adopts | Farmer 2 Does Not Adopt |
| --- | --- | --- |

#### Sequential Representation:

3. Payoffs depend on combined extraction levels and groundwater depth.
#### Justification: This action situation reflects the sequential decision-making process of farmers in extracting groundwater, where the sustainability of the resource depends on the combined actions of all farmers and their observation of each other's behaviors.
