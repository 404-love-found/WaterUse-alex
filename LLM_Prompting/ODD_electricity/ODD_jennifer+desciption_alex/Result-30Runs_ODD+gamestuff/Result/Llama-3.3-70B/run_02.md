### Action Situation 1: Capacitor Adoption Coordination
#### Title: Coordination Dilemma in Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which improve voltage stability, but only if enough neighbors also adopt.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
|  | Farmer 2 Adopt | Farmer 2 Not Adopt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopt** | 3, 3 (Improved voltage stability) | 1, 2 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |
| **Farmer 1 Not Adopt** | 2, 1 (Farmer 2 bears cost, Farmer 1 free-rides) | 0, 0 (No improvement in voltage stability) |
#### Justification: This dilemma reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must coordinate their adoption of capacitors to improve voltage stability, but may choose not to adopt if they expect their neighbors not to adopt, leading to a potential free-rider problem.

### Action Situation 4: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Groundwater Extraction Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, which affects their individual crop yields, but also impacts the collective groundwater level and future extraction costs.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 chooses extraction level (High or Low)
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and chooses extraction level (High or Low)
3. Payoffs depend on individual extraction levels and collective groundwater level
#### Justification: This dilemma reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide how much groundwater to extract, which affects their individual crop yields, but also impacts the collective groundwater level and future extraction costs, leading to a potential tragedy of the commons.
