### Title: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
**Sequential Game Tree:**

```
           [Farmer A]
                 |
         [Decide to Invest/Not Invest]
                 |
     [Farmer B]
           |
     [Decide to Invest/Not Invest]
```

### Justification:
This action situation captures the strategic dilemma faced by farmers sharing the same transformer when deciding whether to invest in capacitors. Capacitors can improve voltage stability and pump efficiency, but the benefits are strongest when adoption is coordinated among farmers. The sequential game tree represents the order in which farmers make their decisions, and the outcome depends on the mutual agreement and coordination. If both farmers invest, the transformer capacity is enhanced, leading to higher reliability. If only one farmer invests, the benefit is weak or hard to attribute, making unilateral investment unattractive.
