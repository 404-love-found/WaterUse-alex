# Run 4 — Qwen/Qwen3.7-Plus

Title: Capacitor Adoption and Transformer Capacity Coordination
Tension: Assurance/Coordination Dilemma (Threshold Public Goods). Farmers must coordinate to invest in capacitors to improve voltage and transformer capacity, but face the risk of bearing private costs without shared benefits if others free-ride.
Matrix: 
| Farmer A \ Farmer B | Invest | Free-Ride |
|---|---|---|
| **Invest** | 3, 3 | 1, 4 |
| **Free-Ride** | 4, 1 | 2, 2 |
Justification: Grounded in the ODD+D description where "a farmer who invests only realises the shared benefit if enough farmers on the same transformer land on 'invest' within the same cycle, otherwise they pay the adoption cost with no return." This captures farmer-farmer coordination, capacitor adoption, and transformer capacity mechanisms under bounded rationality and social learning.

Title: Authorization, Informal Exchange, and Enforcement
Tension: Risky Informal Exchange vs. Formal Compliance. Both farmer and staff must mutually agree to form a collusive tie for informal connection/regularization, balancing mutual gains against the risk of detection, effort costs, and formal penalties.
Matrix: 
| Farmer \ Staff | Collude (Informal) | Comply (Formal) |
|---|---|---|
| **Collude (Informal)** | 3, 3 | 1, 4 |
| **Comply (Formal)** | 4, 1 | 2, 2 |
Justification: Grounded in the text: "a collusive tie forms only where a farmer's offer and their matched staff member's offer agree... willingness depends on their individual corruption level and the farmer's capacity to reciprocate... moderated by the local risk of detection." This captures farmer-staff interaction, authorization/enforcement, and informal exchange mechanisms.
