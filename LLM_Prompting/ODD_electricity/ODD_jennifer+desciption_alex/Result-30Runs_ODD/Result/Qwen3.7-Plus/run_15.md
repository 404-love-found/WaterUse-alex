**Title**: Groundwater Extraction and Aquifer Restraint
**Tension**: Tragedy of the Commons / Prisoner's Dilemma
**Matrix/Sequential Representation**: 
| | Farmer B: Restrain | Farmer B: Full Pump |
|---|---|---|
| **Farmer A: Restrain** | (High Yield, High Yield) | (Low Yield, High Yield) |
| **Farmer A: Full Pump** | (High Yield, Low Yield) | (Med Yield, Med Yield) |
*(Note: Full Pump dominates Restrain individually, but mutual Restrain yields the highest collective payoff, reflecting aquifer stress dynamics).*
**Justification**: Grounded in the text where connected farmers choose between pumping at full rate and restraining extraction, with the attractiveness of restraint rising as aquifer stress increases, reflecting a classic commons dilemma.

**Title**: Transformer Capacity Investment and Regularization
**Tension**: Sequential Bargaining / Hold-up Problem
**Matrix/Sequential Representation**: 
1. **Staff decides**: [Invest Capacity] or [Not Invest]
2. **If [Invest], Farmer decides**: [Accept Regularization] or [Reject]
*Payoffs*:
- (Invest, Accept) → (Staff: -Workload + Reciprocity, Farmer: +Capacity - Fee)
- (Invest, Reject) → (Staff: -Workload, Farmer: +Capacity)
- (Not Invest, *) → (Staff: 0, Farmer: 0)
**Justification**: Grounded in the text describing the staff member's decision to invest transformer capacity for a tied farmer (which declines with workload) and the farmer's subsequent willingness to accept formal regularization (which is comparatively low and independent of workload).
