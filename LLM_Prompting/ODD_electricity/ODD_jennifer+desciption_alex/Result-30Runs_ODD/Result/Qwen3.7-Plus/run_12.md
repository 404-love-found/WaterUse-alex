# Run 12 — Qwen/Qwen3.7-Plus

Title: Capacitor/DSM Adoption Coordination
Tension: Coordination/Assurance dilemma. A farmer investing in a capacitor only realizes the shared benefit if a threshold of co-farmers on the same transformer also invest. If the threshold isn't met, the investor bears the full cost with no return, creating a tension between individual financial risk and collective grid improvement.
Matrix/Sequential Representation: [Normal Form Game: Farmer A vs. Farmer B (representing threshold coordination)] Strategies: {Invest, Not Invest}. Payoffs (Farmer A, Farmer B): (Invest, Invest) = (3, 3); (Invest, Not Invest) = (1, 2); (Not Invest, Invest) = (2, 1); (Not Invest, Not Invest) = (2, 2). Ordinal payoffs reflect that mutual investment yields the highest shared benefit, while unilateral investment is the worst outcome due to sunk costs with no return.
Justification: Grounded in III.iv.a ("a farmer who invests only realises the shared benefit if enough farmers on the same transformer land on 'invest'... otherwise they pay the adoption cost with no return"). Reflects farmer-farmer coordination, capacitor adoption, and bounded rationality where returns hinge on the unobserved intentions of others.

Title: Connection Authorization and Collusive Tie Formation
Tension: Sequential informal exchange and enforcement dilemma. A disconnected farmer chooses between a paid formal connection or an informal one. If informal, a collusive tie only forms if the utility staff independently agrees, balancing their corruption level and detection risk against the farmer's financial strain.
Matrix/Sequential Representation: [Sequential Game Tree] 1. Farmer moves: {Seek Formal, Seek Informal}. 2. If {Seek Formal}: Game ends. Payoffs (Farmer, Staff) = (2, 2) [Formal fee paid, no collusion risk]. 3. If {Seek Informal}: Staff moves: {Collude, Enforce}. - If {Collude}: Payoffs = (4, 3) [Farmer gets cheaper informal access; Staff gets reciprocal benefit/bribe]. - If {Enforce}: Payoffs = (1, 1) [Farmer penalized/rejected; Staff bears enforcement effort cost but avoids detection risk].
Justification: Grounded in III.iv.a ("Each disconnected farmer chooses between pursuing a paid, formal connection or remaining informal... a collusive tie forms only when both sides are independently willing"). Reflects farmer-staff interaction, authorization/enforcement, informal exchange, and the conditional nature of collusion based on financial strain and oversight risk.
