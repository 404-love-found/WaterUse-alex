**Notes on the presentation**

* **Ordinal payoffs** are used because the ODD + D explicitly states that “the precise ordinal structure of each choice is intentionally left out of this description.” Ranks 1 (best) → 3 (worst) illustrate the direction of incentives without assigning numeric utilities.  
* **Simultaneous games** capture the “offers must match” or “threshold‑dependent” coordination described in the text.  
* **Sequential trees** are provided where the ODD + D specifies a clear ordering (e.g., staff first offers informal terms, then farmer decides; staff enforcement precedes farmer compliance; capacity upgrade precedes stochastic burnout).  
* All ten action‑situations are **directly grounded** in the ODD + D narrative (process overview, sub‑models, and design‑concept sections). No invented situations are introduced.
