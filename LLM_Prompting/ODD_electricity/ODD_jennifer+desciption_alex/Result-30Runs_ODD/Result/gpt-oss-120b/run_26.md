| 3 | **Collusion‑tie formation (Farmer ↔ Staff)** | Both parties may **enter a collusive exchange** (bribe / reciprocal service) or **stay clean**. | 4 | **Staff capacity‑investment for a tied farmer (Staff → Farmer)** | After a collusive tie exists, the staff decides **whether to invest** in additional transformer capacity on behalf of the farmer (or a group). | 5 | **Groundwater‑extraction choice (Farmer ↔ Farmer)** | Each farmer can **extract at full rate** (high short‑term profit, high aquifer stress) or **restrain** (lower profit, preserves the aquifer). | 6 | **Enforcement vs compliance (Staff ↔ Farmer)** | Staff may **enforce** the authorised‑connection rule (monitor, impose fines) or **ignore**. | 7 | **Social‑learning / imitation (Farmer ↔ Neighbor‑Farmer)** | After observing a neighbour’s successful capacitor adoption, a farmer decides **to imitate** (invest) or **to wait**. | 8 | **Threshold‑opening of the wider imitation pool (Transformer‑level coordination)** | Once a transformer’s **adoption count jumps by a threshold** in a single cycle, the **imitation pool opens** for all other farmers on that transformer. The decision is whether to **trigger** the threshold (by coordinating a burst of investments) or **stay below** (avoid collective risk).
