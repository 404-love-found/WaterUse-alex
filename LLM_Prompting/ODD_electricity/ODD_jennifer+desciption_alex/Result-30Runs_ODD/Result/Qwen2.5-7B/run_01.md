```
           / \
          /   \
    Adopt   Not Adopt
         /     \
        /       \
  [Farmer 2]  [Farmer 2]
     / \        / \
    /   \      /   \
Adopt  Not Adopt Adopt  Not Adopt
```

### Justification:
This action situation captures the dilemma faced by farmers in deciding whether to invest in capacitors (a shared resource) when the adoption of capacitors depends on the collective action of farmers on the same transformer. The sequential nature of the game highlights the dependency of the outcome on the decisions of other farmers, reflecting the strategic interdependence and bounded rationality of the farmers.
