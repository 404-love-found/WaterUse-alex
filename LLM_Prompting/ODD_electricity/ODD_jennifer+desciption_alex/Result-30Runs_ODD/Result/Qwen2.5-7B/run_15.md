### Title: Farmer-Farmer Coordination to Adopt Capacitors

### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination to Adopt Capacitors

### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
**Sequential Representation (Game Tree):**
```
          [Farmer 1]
            |
     [Farmer 2]  -- Invest
            |     -- Do Not Invest
            |
          [Farmer 1]
            |
     [Farmer 2]  -- Invest
            |     -- Do Not Invest
            |
            ...
```

### Justification:
In the model, the adoption of capacitors is a collective action problem where farmers must coordinate to achieve a shared benefit. Each farmer has to decide whether to invest in a capacitor, which provides a shared benefit if enough farmers adopt them. However, each farmer bears the cost of the investment individually, creating a dilemma where the farmer might choose not to invest if they believe others will do so. The sequential game tree represents the repeated nature of the decision-making process, where each farmer must consider the potential actions of their neighbors in the previous cycle.
