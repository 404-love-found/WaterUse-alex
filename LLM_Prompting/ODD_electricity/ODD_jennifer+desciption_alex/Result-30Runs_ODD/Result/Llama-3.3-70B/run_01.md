### Action Situation 1: Farmer Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Coordination Dilemma
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 1 Invests | Farmer 1 Does Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 2 Invests** | 3, 3 | 0, 2 |
| **Farmer 2 Does Not Invest** | 2, 0 | 1, 1 |

#### Justification: Farmers face a coordination dilemma when deciding to invest in capacitors. The payoff matrix represents the strategic tension where both farmers benefit if they both invest (3, 3), but if only one invests, they incur a cost without benefit (0, 2 or 2, 0), and if neither invests, they incur a lower cost but also no benefit (1, 1).

### Action Situation 4: Authorization and Enforcement
#### Title: Compliance and Monitoring
#### Tension: Inspection Game
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer decides to authorize or not.
2. Staff decides to monitor or not.
3. Payoffs:
   - If farmer authorizes and staff monitors: 2, 2
   - If farmer authorizes and staff does not monitor: 3, 1
   - If farmer does not authorize and staff monitors: 0, 3
   - If farmer does not authorize and staff does not monitor: 1, 1

#### Justification: The farmer and staff engage in an inspection game where the farmer decides whether to authorize their connection, and the staff decides whether to monitor. The sequential representation captures the strategic tension where the farmer weighs the cost of authorization against the risk of being caught without authorization, and the staff weighs the cost of monitoring against the benefit of detecting unauthorized connections.

### Action Situation 5: Social Learning and Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Imitation and Adoption
#### Tension: Threshold Model of Collective Behavior
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer observes neighbors' adoption decisions.
2. Farmer decides to adopt or not based on observed behavior and personal threshold.
3. Payoffs:
   - If farmer adopts and enough neighbors adopt: 3
   - If farmer adopts and not enough neighbors adopt: 0
   - If farmer does not adopt: 1

#### Justification: Farmers engage in social learning, observing their neighbors' adoption decisions and deciding whether to adopt based on a personal threshold. The sequential representation captures the strategic tension where farmers weigh the benefit of adopting against the cost, considering the likelihood of enough neighbors adopting to make the investment worthwhile.
