### Action Situation 1: Farmer Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which improve power quality, but only if enough neighbors also invest.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Invest | Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invest** | 3, 3 | 0, 2 |
| **Not Invest** | 2, 0 | 1, 1 |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide whether to invest in capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that if both farmers invest, they both receive a high payoff (3), but if only one invests, the investor receives a low payoff (0) while the non-investor receives a moderate payoff (2).

### Action Situation 3: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Groundwater Extraction Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, which affects their own payoffs as well as the payoffs of other farmers.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 chooses extraction level (High or Low).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and chooses extraction level (High or Low).
3. Payoffs are determined by the combined extraction levels.

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide how much groundwater to extract. The sequential representation shows that each farmer's decision depends on the other farmer's choice, and the payoffs are determined by the combined extraction levels.

### Action Situation 5: Social Learning and Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Social Learning for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to adopt capacitors based on social learning from their neighbors.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 adopts a capacitor or not.
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and decides whether to adopt a capacitor.
3. Payoffs are determined by the number of farmers who adopt capacitors.

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide whether to adopt capacitors based on social learning. The sequential representation shows that each farmer's decision depends on the previous farmer's choice, and the payoffs are determined by the number of farmers who adopt capacitors.
