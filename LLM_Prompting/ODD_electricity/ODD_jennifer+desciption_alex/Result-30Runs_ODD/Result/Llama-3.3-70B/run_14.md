#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which benefit all farmers on the same transformer, but the cost is borne individually.

### Action Situation 3: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Groundwater Extraction Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, balancing their individual needs against the collective risk of aquifer depletion.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 chooses extraction level (High or Low).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and chooses extraction level (High or Low).
3. Payoffs are determined by the combined extraction levels and aquifer stress.
#### Justification: The sequential representation reflects the dynamic nature of groundwater extraction, where each farmer's decision is influenced by the actions of others and the state of the aquifer.

### Action Situation 5: Transformer Capacity Investment
#### Title: Investment in Transformer Capacity
#### Tension: Staff and farmers must decide whether to invest in transformer capacity, which can improve power quality but also requires significant upfront costs.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Staff decides whether to invest in transformer capacity (Invest or Not Invest).
2. Farmers observe staff's decision and choose whether to contribute to the investment (Contribute or Not Contribute).
3. Payoffs are determined by the level of investment and the resulting power quality.
#### Justification: The sequential representation reflects the dynamic nature of investment decisions, where staff and farmers must coordinate their actions to achieve improved power quality.
