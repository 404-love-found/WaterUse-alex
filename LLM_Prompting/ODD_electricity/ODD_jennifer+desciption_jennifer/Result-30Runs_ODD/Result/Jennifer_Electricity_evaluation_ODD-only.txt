  TP [AS4: Mutual-exchange coordination game between farmer and sub-station staff] L48: Collusion Exchange Game. Players: Farmer and Staff. Actions: Farmer offers collusion (Offer/Not Offer), Staff accepts (Accept/Reject). Or both decide to collude or not. The ODD+D: "a collusive tie forms only when both sides are independently willing." So it's a mutual agreement. If both collude, they gain from informal exchanges: farmer gets lenient enforcement, unauthorized connection, etc., staff gets bribes or favors. If one offers and other rejects, the offerer may face risk of exposure or nothing. If neither colludes, formal rules apply. So it's a trust game-like: farmer's offer is risky if staff rejects. Payoffs: Both collude: farmer gets high benefit (3), staff gets high (3) (bribe). Farmer offers, staff rejects: farmer may get punished or nothing, staff gets maybe 2 (no bribe but no risk). Farmer doesn't offer, staff would accept: staff gets 1 (no bribe, maybe disappointed), farmer gets 1 (no benefit). Neither: farmer 1, staff 1 (formal). Need to reflect asymmetry: staff has power to enforce. So if farmer offers and staff rejects, staff might report or just refuse, farmer might face penalty. So farmer's payoff could be 0 (worst). Staff's payoff if rejecting an offer could be 2 (no bribe but maintains integrity). If farmer doesn't offer and staff would accept, staff gets 1 (missed opportunity), farmer gets 1. So matrix
