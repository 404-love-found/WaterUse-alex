### Title: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
**Sequential Game Tree:**

```
                   Farmer A
                      |
     Farmer B (Invest)  /   \ (Not Invest)
                      /       \
       Farmer A (Invest)       Farmer A (Not Invest)
```

### Justification:
This action situation represents the challenge faced by farmers in coordinating to invest in capacitors. Each farmer must decide whether to invest in a capacitor, but the benefit of doing so is only realized if enough farmers in their transformer group also invest. This creates a classic coordination problem where individual farmers have an incentive to "free-ride" on the contributions of others, leading to a potential failure of collective action.
