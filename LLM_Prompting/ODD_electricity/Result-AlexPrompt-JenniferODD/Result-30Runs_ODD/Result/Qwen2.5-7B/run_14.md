### Action Situation 1: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

**Tension:** Farmer-Farmer Coordination

**Matrix/Sequential Representation:**
- **Sequential Game Tree Representation:**

```
1. Farmer 1 decides whether to adopt a capacitor:
   - Adopt
   - Do Not Adopt

2. Farmer 2 decides whether to adopt a capacitor:
   - Adopt
   - Do Not Adopt

Payoffs (Adopt, Adopt): (2, 2)
Payoffs (Adopt, Do Not Adopt): (1, 0)
Payoffs (Do Not Adopt, Adopt): (0, 1)
Payoffs (Do Not Adopt, Do Not Adopt): (1, 1)
```

**Justification:** Farmers must coordinate to achieve mutual benefits through capacitor adoption. If both farmers adopt, they share the benefits. If only one adopts, they do not benefit. If neither adopts, the benefits are lower. The sequential representation captures the timing of decisions and the conditional nature of benefits.
