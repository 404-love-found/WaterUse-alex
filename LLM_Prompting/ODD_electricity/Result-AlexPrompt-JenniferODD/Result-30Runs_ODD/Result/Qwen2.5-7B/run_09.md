### Title: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
**Simultaneous Normal-Form Payoff Matrix:**

|                  | Farmer 2: Invest | Farmer 2: Do Not Invest |
|------------------|------------------|------------------------|
| **Farmer 1: Invest** | (B, B)            | (A, C)                  |
| **Farmer 1: Do Not Invest** | (C, A)           | (D, D)                  |

- **A**: Farmer 1 pays the adoption cost with no return.
- **B**: Shared benefit from successful adoption if enough farmers invest.
- **C**: Farmer 1 gets no benefit, pays the adoption cost.
- **D**: No shared benefit, no costs.

#### Justification:
This action situation captures the dilemma faced by farmers in deciding whether to invest in capacitors. The shared benefit only materializes if a critical mass of farmers invest, creating a coordination problem. Farmers must coordinate their actions to achieve the shared benefit, leading to the potential for free-riding. The matrix reflects the strategic tension between individual costs if the threshold is not met and the potential collective benefit if the threshold is achieved.


#### Justification:
