### Action Situation 1: Farmer Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which benefit all farmers on the same transformer, but the cost is borne individually.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 1 Invests | Farmer 1 Does Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 2 Invests** | 3, 3 (both benefit) | 0, 2 (only Farmer 2 benefits) |
| **Farmer 2 Does Not Invest** | 2, 0 (only Farmer 1 benefits) | 0, 0 (no benefit) |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide whether to invest in capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that if both farmers invest, they both benefit (payoff 3), but if only one invests, the other free-rides (payoff 2 for the non-investing farmer, 0 for the investing farmer).

### Action Situation 3: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Groundwater Extraction Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, given the risk of aquifer depletion and the potential for future restrictions.
#### Sequential Representation:

1. Farmer 1 chooses extraction level (high or low).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and chooses extraction level (high or low).
3. Payoffs are determined by the combined extraction level and the resulting aquifer depletion.

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide how much groundwater to extract. The sequential representation shows that each farmer's decision depends on the other farmer's choice, and the payoffs are determined by the combined extraction level and the resulting aquifer depletion.

#### Title: Authorization and Enforcement Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to pay for authorized connections, while staff must decide whether to enforce formal rules or accept informal exchanges.

|  | Staff Enforces Formal Rules | Staff Accepts Informal Exchange |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers and staff who must decide whether to comply with formal rules or engage in informal exchanges.
