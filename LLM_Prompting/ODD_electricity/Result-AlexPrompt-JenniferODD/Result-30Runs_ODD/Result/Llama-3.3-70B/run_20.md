### Action Situation 1: Farmer Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which benefit all farmers on the same transformer, but the cost is borne individually.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
|  | Farmer 1 Invests | Farmer 1 Does Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 2 Invests** | 3, 3 | 0, 2 |
| **Farmer 2 Does Not Invest** | 2, 0 | 1, 1 |
#### Justification: This situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide whether to invest in capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that if both farmers invest, they both receive a higher payoff (3), but if only one invests, the investor receives a lower payoff (2) while the non-investor receives a higher payoff (0 or 2), creating a free-rider problem.

### Action Situation 3: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Groundwater Extraction Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, which affects the aquifer level and the energy cost of extraction.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 chooses extraction level (high or low).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and chooses extraction level (high or low).
3. Payoffs are determined by the combined extraction level and the resulting aquifer level.
#### Justification: This situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide how much groundwater to extract. The sequential representation shows that each farmer's choice affects the payoffs of both farmers, creating a situation where the first farmer's choice influences the second farmer's decision.

### Action Situation 5: Social Learning and Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Social Learning for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to adopt capacitors based on observations of their neighbors' adoption decisions and outcomes.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 adopts or does not adopt a capacitor.
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and outcome.
3. Farmer 2 decides whether to adopt a capacitor based on the observed outcome.
#### Justification: This situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide whether to adopt capacitors based on social learning. The sequential representation shows that each farmer's choice is influenced by the observed outcomes of their neighbors, creating a situation where the first farmer's choice affects the second farmer's decision.
