### Action Situation 1: Farmer Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which improve power quality, but only if enough neighbors also invest.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 1 Invests | Farmer 1 Does Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 2 Invests** | 3, 3 (Improved power quality) | 0, 2 (No improvement) |
| **Farmer 2 Does Not Invest** | 2, 0 (No improvement) | 1, 1 (Status quo) |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must coordinate their investments in capacitors to achieve improved power quality.

### Action Situation 4: Farmer Authorization and Enforcement
#### Title: Compliance with Authorization Rules
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to pay for authorized connections, while staff decide whether to enforce these rules.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 chooses to pay for authorization or not.
2. If Farmer 1 pays, Staff decides whether to enforce the rules (always comply).
3. If Farmer 1 does not pay, Staff decides whether to detect and penalize or not.

#### Justification: This action situation captures the sequential strategic interaction between farmers and staff regarding authorization and enforcement of electricity connections.

### Action Situation 5: Social Learning for Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Imitation and Adoption of Capacitors
#### Tension: Farmers observe their neighbors' adoption decisions and outcomes, influencing their own decision to adopt capacitors.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 observes neighbors' capacitor adoption outcomes.
2. Farmer 1 decides whether to adopt a capacitor based on observed outcomes and personal experience.

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the process of social learning, where farmers update their decisions based on the observed experiences of their neighbors, influencing the diffusion of capacitor adoption.
