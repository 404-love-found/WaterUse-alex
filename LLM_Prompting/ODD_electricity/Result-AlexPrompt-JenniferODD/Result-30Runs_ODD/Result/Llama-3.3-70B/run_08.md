#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, considering both their individual needs and the collective impact on the aquifer.
#### Sequential Representation:

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between individual interests and collective well-being in groundwater extraction, as farmers consider the potential consequences of their actions on the shared resource.

### Action Situation 5: Authorization and Enforcement
#### Title: Formal Connection Decision
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to pursue a formal connection, considering the costs and benefits of authorized access to electricity.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer chooses to apply for formal connection or remain informal.

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between the benefits of formal access to electricity and the costs of authorization, as farmers weigh the advantages of secure access against the costs and staff consider the trade-offs between enforcement and informal exchange.
