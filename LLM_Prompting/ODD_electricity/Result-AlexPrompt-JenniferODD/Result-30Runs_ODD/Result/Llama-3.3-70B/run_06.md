### Action Situation 1: Farmer Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Coordination Dilemma
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 1 Invests | Farmer 1 Does Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 2 Invests** | 3, 3 | 0, 2 |
| **Farmer 2 Does Not Invest** | 2, 0 | 1, 1 |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between two farmers deciding whether to invest in capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that if both farmers invest (3, 3), they both benefit from improved power quality. If only one invests (0, 2 or 2, 0), the investor bears the cost without the benefit, while the non-investor free-rides. If neither invests (1, 1), they both suffer from poor power quality but avoid the investment cost.

### Action Situation 4: Staff Enforcement
#### Title: Enforcement Dilemma
#### Tension: Monitoring and Compliance
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Staff decides to enforce or not.
2. If enforce, farmer decides to comply or not.
   - Compliance: Staff gains (enforcement success), Farmer loses (penalty).
   - Non-compliance: Staff loses (enforcement failure), Farmer gains (avoided penalty).
3. If not enforce, farmer decides to abide by rules or not.
   - Abide: Both staff and farmer gain (reduced tension).
   - Not abide: Staff loses (missed enforcement opportunity), Farmer gains (unauthorized benefits).

#### Justification: This sequential action situation reflects the strategic tension between staff enforcement decisions and farmer compliance. The staff must weigh the costs and benefits of enforcement, considering the farmer's potential compliance or non-compliance. The farmer, in turn, decides based on the staff's action, balancing the risks and benefits of complying with rules or exploiting non-enforcement.

### Action Situation 6: Authorization and Informal Connections
#### Title: Formal vs. Informal Connections
#### Tension: Access and Penalty Dilemma
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer decides to seek formal authorization or an informal connection.
2. If formal, staff decides to grant or deny authorization.
   - Grant: Farmer gains (legal access), Staff gains (official revenue).
   - Deny: Farmer loses (no access), Staff loses (missed revenue opportunity).
3. If informal, staff decides to detect and penalize or not.
   - Detect and penalize: Farmer loses (penalty), Staff gains (enforcement success).
   - Not detect: Farmer gains (unauthorized access), Staff loses (enforcement failure).

#### Justification: This sequential action situation reflects the strategic tension between a farmer's decision to seek formal or informal electricity connections and the staff's subsequent actions. The farmer weighs the benefits and risks of formal authorization against those of informal connections, considering the staff's potential responses. The staff, in turn, decides based on detection and penalty strategies, balancing enforcement with potential revenue or relationships.
