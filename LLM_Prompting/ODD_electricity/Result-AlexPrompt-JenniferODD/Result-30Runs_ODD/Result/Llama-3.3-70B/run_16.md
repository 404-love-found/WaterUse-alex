### Action Situation 3: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Tragedy of the Commons in Groundwater Use
#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, facing a trade-off between individual gain and collective over-extraction, which degrades the aquifer.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

Since this situation involves multiple farmers and a sequential decision process based on observed outcomes and social learning, a compact sequential representation is more appropriate:

1. Farmer 1 chooses extraction level (High or Low).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and outcomes, then chooses extraction level.
3. Payoffs are determined by the combined extraction levels, with over-extraction leading to decreased payoffs for both.

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension in groundwater extraction, where individual farmers' decisions to maximize their water use can lead to collective over-extraction and degradation of the aquifer.
