### Action Situation 1: Farmer Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which improve power quality, but only if enough neighbors also invest.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
|  | Invest | Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invest** | 3, 3 | 0, 4 |
| **Not Invest** | 4, 0 | 1, 1 |
#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide whether to invest in capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that if both farmers invest, they both receive a higher payoff (3), but if only one invests, the other free-rides and receives a higher payoff (4), while the investor receives 0.

### Action Situation 3: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Groundwater Extraction and Restraint
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to extract groundwater at full rate or restrain extraction, considering the energy cost of extraction and potential taxes.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 chooses to extract at full rate or restrain.
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's choice and chooses to extract at full rate or restrain.
3. Payoffs are determined by the combined extraction choices and aquifer stress.
#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must decide whether to extract groundwater. The sequential representation shows that farmers make decisions based on the observed choices of others and the state of the aquifer.

### Action Situation 5: Transformer Capacity Investment
#### Title: Transformer Capacity Investment
#### Tension: Staff must decide whether to invest in transformer capacity on behalf of tied farmers, considering workload and risk of detection.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Staff chooses to invest in transformer capacity or not.
2. Farmer observes staff's choice and chooses to accept formal regularisation or not.
