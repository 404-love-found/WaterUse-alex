#### Title: Collective Action for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Coordination vs. Free-Riding
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
|  | Farmer 2 Adopts | Farmer 2 Doesn't Adopt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopts** | 3, 3 (Improved voltage stability for both) | 1, 4 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |

The payoff matrix shows the benefits of coordinated adoption (3, 3) versus the temptation to free-ride (4, 1 or 1, 4), highlighting the challenge of achieving collective action.

### Action Situation 3: Sequential Decision on Transformer Capacity Contribution
#### Title: Contribution to Transformer Capacity
#### Tension: Contribution vs. Free-Riding
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 decides to contribute (C) or not (NC) to transformer capacity upgrade.
2. If Farmer 1 contributes (C), Farmer 2 decides to contribute (C) or not (NC).
   - If Farmer 2 contributes (C), both enjoy improved reliability (3, 3).
   - If Farmer 2 doesn't contribute (NC), Farmer 1 bears the cost, and Farmer 2 free-rides (1, 4).
3. If Farmer 1 doesn't contribute (NC), the outcome depends on Farmer 2's decision:
   - If Farmer 2 contributes (C), Farmer 2 bears the cost, and Farmer 1 free-rides (4, 1).
   - If Farmer 2 doesn't contribute (NC), both face reduced reliability (2, 2).
#### Justification: This sequential action situation reflects the decision-making process regarding contributions to transformer capacity upgrades. It highlights the strategic tension between contributing to improve reliability and the temptation to free-ride on others' contributions.

### Action Situation 5: Staff Enforcement Decision
#### Title: Enforcement of Formal Rules
#### Tension: Compliance vs. Leniency
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Staff decides to enforce (E) or not enforce (NE) formal rules.
2. If Staff enforces (E):
   - Farmers comply, and Staff maintains reputation (3, 3).
   - Farmers resist, leading to conflict and potential penalties (1, 2).
3. If Staff doesn't enforce (NE):
   - Farmers may comply voluntarily, and Staff saves effort (2, 4).
   - Farmers may exploit leniency, leading to overuse and system strain (4, 1).
#### Justification: This sequential action situation captures the staff's decision on whether to enforce formal rules. It reflects the tension between ensuring compliance and the potential benefits and drawbacks of leniency, considering the farmers' likely responses to enforcement decisions.
