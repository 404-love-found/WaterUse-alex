### Action Situation 1: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which improve voltage stability, but only if enough neighbors also invest.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 2 Invests | Farmer 2 Does Not Invest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Invests** | 3, 3 (improved voltage stability) | 1, 2 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |
| **Farmer 1 Does Not Invest** | 2, 1 (Farmer 2 bears cost, Farmer 1 free-rides) | 0, 0 (no improvement in voltage stability) |

#### Justification: This situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers who must coordinate their investments in capacitors to achieve improved voltage stability, a collective benefit.

### Action Situation 2: Farmer-Staff Interaction on Informal Exchange
#### Title: Informal Access to Electricity
#### Tension: Farmers and staff must decide whether to engage in informal exchange (tolerance of unauthorized access) or adhere to formal rules.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Staff Tolerates | Staff Enforces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer Offers Informal Cooperation** | 3, 3 (mutual benefit) | 0, 2 (Farmer loses, Staff gains from enforcement) |
| **Farmer Complies Formally** | 2, 1 (Farmer pays fee, Staff has less effort) | 1, 0 (Farmer pays fee, Staff has enforcement effort) |

#### Justification: This situation highlights the strategic tension between farmers seeking cheaper access and staff deciding between enforcement effort and informal tolerance, influenced by trust and oversight risk.

### Action Situation 3: Sequential Decision on Transformer Capacity Contribution
#### Title: Contribution to Transformer Capacity
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer decides to contribute to transformer capacity upgrade.
2. If contributed, Staff decides whether to invest in maintenance.
   - If Staff invests, outcome is (3, 3) for reliable electricity.
   - If Staff does not invest, outcome is (1, 2) for Farmer, as Farmer bears cost without full benefit.
3. If Farmer does not contribute, Staff decides on enforcement or tolerance.
   - Enforcement leads to (0, 2) if Farmer is caught, (2, 1) if not.
   - Tolerance leads to (2, 3) if Farmer and Staff cooperate informally.

#### Justification: This sequential situation reflects the strategic tension and timing of decisions between farmers contributing to transformer capacity and staff deciding on maintenance or enforcement, considering the outcomes of these interactions.

#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, considering the trade-off between short-term benefits and long-term sustainability.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 2 Extracts High | Farmer 2 Extracts Low |
| --- | --- | --- |

#### Justification: This situation represents the strategic tension between farmers who must balance individual extraction decisions with the collective sustainability of the groundwater resource, reflecting a classic tragedy of the commons dilemma.
