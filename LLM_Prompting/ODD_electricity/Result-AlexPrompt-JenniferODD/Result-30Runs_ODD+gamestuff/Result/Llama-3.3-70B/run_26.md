#### Title: Collective Action for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Coordination Dilemma
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 2 Adopts Capacitor | Farmer 2 Does Not Adopt Capacitor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopts Capacitor** | 3, 3 (Improved voltage stability) | 1, 2 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |
| **Farmer 1 Does Not Adopt Capacitor** | 2, 1 (Farmer 2 bears cost, Farmer 1 free-rides) | 0, 0 (No improvement in voltage stability) |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between two farmers deciding whether to adopt a capacitor to improve voltage stability. The payoff matrix shows that mutual adoption leads to the best outcome, but unilateral adoption can result in one farmer bearing the cost while the other free-rides.
