### Action Situation 1: Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Coordination for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors to improve voltage stability, considering their neighbors' potential adoption.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
|  | Farmer 2 Adopts | Farmer 2 Does Not Adopt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopts** | 3, 3 (Improved voltage stability) | 1, 2 (Farmer 1 bears cost alone) |
| **Farmer 1 Does Not Adopt** | 2, 1 (Farmer 2 bears cost alone) | 0, 0 (No improvement) |
#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between farmers in deciding whether to adopt capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that coordinated adoption leads to improved voltage stability, while unilateral adoption results in one farmer bearing the cost without full benefit.
