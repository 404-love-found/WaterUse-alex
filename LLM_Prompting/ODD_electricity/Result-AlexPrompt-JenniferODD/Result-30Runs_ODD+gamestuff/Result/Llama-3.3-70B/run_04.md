### Action Situation 1: Capacitor Adoption Coordination
#### Title: Coordination Dilemma in Capacitor Adoption
#### Tension: Farmers must decide whether to invest in capacitors, which improve voltage stability, but only if enough neighbors also adopt.
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 2 Adopt | Farmer 2 Not Adopt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopt** | 3, 3 (Improved voltage stability) | 1, 2 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |
| **Farmer 1 Not Adopt** | 2, 1 (Farmer 2 bears cost, Farmer 1 free-rides) | 0, 0 (No cost or benefit) |

#### Justification: This dilemma reflects the strategic tension between farmers' decisions to adopt capacitors. If both adopt, they both benefit from improved voltage stability. If one adopts and the other does not, the adopter bears the cost without the full benefit, while the non-adopter free-rides on the improved conditions.

#### Title: Informal Access Dilemma

|  | Staff Tolerate | Staff Enforce |

### Action Situation 4: Groundwater Extraction
#### Title: Groundwater Extraction Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers must decide how much groundwater to extract, considering the trade-off between short-term benefits and long-term sustainability.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 extracts water (High or Low).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's action and decides extraction level (High or Low).
3. Payoffs are determined by the combined extraction levels, with high extraction leading to short-term gains but long-term depletion and increased costs.

#### Justification: This action situation captures the dynamic of farmers making decisions about groundwater extraction, influenced by observations of their neighbors' actions and considering both immediate benefits and future consequences.

### Action Situation 6: Social Learning and Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Social Learning Dilemma
#### Tension: Farmers observe their neighbors' adoption of capacitors and must decide whether to imitate, considering the potential for improved voltage stability but also the risk of failed or isolated adoption.
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer 1 adopts a capacitor (or not).
2. Farmer 2 observes Farmer 1's outcome and decides whether to adopt.
3. Payoffs depend on the success of the adoption and the decision to imitate or not.

#### Justification: This action situation captures the process of social learning in the context of capacitor adoption. Farmers learn from their neighbors' experiences and make decisions based on observed outcomes, influencing the diffusion of the technology.
