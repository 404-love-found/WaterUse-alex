### Action Situation 1: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption
#### Title: Collective Action for Voltage Stability
#### Tension: Coordination dilemma among farmers to adopt capacitors for improved voltage stability
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Farmer 2 Adopts Capacitor | Farmer 2 Does Not Adopt Capacitor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer 1 Adopts Capacitor** | 3, 3 (Improved voltage stability) | 1, 2 (Farmer 1 bears cost, Farmer 2 free-rides) |
| **Farmer 1 Does Not Adopt Capacitor** | 2, 1 (Farmer 2 bears cost, Farmer 1 free-rides) | 0, 0 (No improvement in voltage stability) |

#### Justification: This action situation reflects the strategic tension between two farmers deciding whether to adopt capacitors. The payoff matrix shows that both adopting capacitors leads to the best outcome for both farmers (improved voltage stability), but if one adopts and the other does not, the adopter bears the cost while the other free-rides.

### Action Situation 2: Farmer-Staff Interaction on Informal Exchange
#### Title: Informal Exchange and Enforcement
#### Tension: Dilemma between farmers and staff on informal exchange versus enforcement
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:

|  | Staff Enforces Formal Rules | Staff Tolerates Informal Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Farmer Seeks Informal Access** | 0, 2 (Farmer penalized, Staff maintains formal rule) | 3, 3 (Farmer gets cheap access, Staff gets reciprocal benefit) |
| **Farmer Complies with Formal Rules** | 2, 1 (Farmer pays formal fee, Staff bears effort cost) | 1, 0 (Farmer pays formal fee, Staff does not tolerate informal access) |

#### Justification: This action situation captures the strategic interaction between a farmer and staff regarding informal access to electricity. The matrix shows that mutual cooperation (informal access and tolerance) leads to a beneficial outcome for both, but if the staff enforces formal rules while the farmer seeks informal access, the farmer is penalized.

### Action Situation 5: Sequential Farmer-Staff Interaction on Authorization and Maintenance
#### Title: Authorization and Maintenance
#### Tension: Sequential dilemma between a farmer and staff on authorization and maintenance
#### Sequential Representation:
1. Farmer decides to seek formal authorization or informal access.
2. If farmer seeks formal authorization, staff decides whether to invest in maintenance.
   - If staff invests in maintenance, outcomes are (3, 2) for (farmer, staff), reflecting improved reliability and staff effort.
   - If staff does not invest in maintenance, outcomes are (1, 1), reflecting unreliability and lack of effort.
3. If farmer seeks informal access, staff decides whether to tolerate or enforce formal rules.
   - If staff tolerates, outcomes are (2, 3), reflecting cheap access for the farmer and reciprocal benefit for staff.
   - If staff enforces, outcomes are (0, 2), reflecting penalty for the farmer and maintenance of formal rules by staff.

#### Justification: This sequential action situation captures the dynamic interaction between a farmer and staff regarding authorization and maintenance. The farmer's initial decision influences the staff's subsequent choices, reflecting the sequential nature of their interaction and the resulting outcomes based on their decisions.
