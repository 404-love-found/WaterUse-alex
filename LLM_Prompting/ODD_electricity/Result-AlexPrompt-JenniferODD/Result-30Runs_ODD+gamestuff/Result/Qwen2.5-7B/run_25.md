### Title: Capacitor Adoption Coordination

### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination to Invest in Capacitors
#### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
**Sequential Game Tree:**

```
          (Farmer 1)
            |
      [Invest]--(Farmer 2)
            |       |
       [Invest]    [No Invest]
            |       |
       [Invest]    [No Invest]
```

#### Justification:
Farmers share a transformer and face a coordination dilemma. If both farmers invest in capacitors, the transformer capacity is increased, improving voltage stability and pump efficiency. However, if only one farmer invests, the benefit is limited, making unilateral investment less attractive due to the free-rider problem. The sequential game tree captures the timing of decisions and the interdependence between the two farmers.
