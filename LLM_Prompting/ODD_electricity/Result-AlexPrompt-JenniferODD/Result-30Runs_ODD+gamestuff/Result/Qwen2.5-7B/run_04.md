### Title: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination on Capacitor Adoption

### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
**Sequential Game Tree:**

```
         [Farmer A]
           |
           v
[Farmer B]---[Farmer A]
           |
           v
[Farmer B]
```

- **Node 1**: Farmer A decides whether to adopt a capacitor.
  - If Farmer A adopts, Farmer B decides whether to adopt.
  - If Farmer A does not adopt, Farmer B decides whether to adopt.

### Justification:
This action situation captures the strategic tension between two farmers who share a transformer. The adoption of a capacitor by one farmer can improve the reliability of electricity for both, but the benefits are stronger if both farmers adopt. This creates a coordination problem where each farmer must decide whether to invest in the capacitor based on the expected behavior of the other farmer. The sequential nature of the game reflects the order in which farmers make their decisions, as described in the ODD+D text.
