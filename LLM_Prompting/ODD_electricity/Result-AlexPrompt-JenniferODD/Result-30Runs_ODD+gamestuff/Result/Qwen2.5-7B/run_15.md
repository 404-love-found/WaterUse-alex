### Title: Capacitor Adoption Coordination

### Tension: Farmer-Farmer Coordination for Capacitor Adoption

### Matrix/Sequential Representation:
**Normal Form Payoff Matrix:**

| Farmer 2 | No Adoption | Adopt Capacitor |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **No Adoption** | (2, 2) | (1, 4) |
| **Adopt Capacitor** | (4, 1) | (3, 3) |

### Justification:
This action situation represents the strategic tension between farmers deciding whether to invest in capacitors that improve local voltage stability. If both farmers adopt capacitors, they both benefit from improved voltage quality (3, 3). If one farmer adopts and the other does not, the adopter bears the cost while the non-adopter benefits (4, 1). If neither adopts, both face the worst outcome (2, 2). The unilateral adoption can be unattractive due to the free-rider problem, making coordination crucial for mutual benefit.
